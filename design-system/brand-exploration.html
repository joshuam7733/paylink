Brand & design system · v0.2 For final approval

A payment you can see from link to doorstep.

Paylink sits between a seller and a buyer who have never met. The identity has one job: make that stranger on the other side of the screen feel accountable. It does that with a calm navy structure, emerald reserved for the moments that move a deal forward, and a lot of Soft Cloud space so nothing is hidden.

01 · Logo exploration

Three marks explored. Interlock selected.

Each concept is drawn on a 64-unit grid from two shapes at most, so it survives at 16px. All three use only Deep Navy and Paylink Emerald, and swap navy for Soft Cloud on dark backgrounds. The wordmark is lowercase Bricolage Grotesque with an emerald tittle on the i, which ties every concept back to the same green point.

03 · Typography

A warm display face over a precise working face

Bricolage Grotesque sets headlines and amounts. Its optical sizing makes it slightly characterful when large and plain when small, which is where the "approachable" comes from. Instrument Sans does all of the reading and form work and has tabular figures for money columns. IBM Plex Mono is used only for references like PL-7QK2M9 and for the demo label. All three are free on Google Fonts.

06 · Motion

Movement that confirms, never decorates

Motion is used to say "that worked" or "this changed". Everything here runs on four durations and one easing curve. In the app they're implemented with Motion for React using the same values. Try each demo, then switch on reduced motion to see the fallback.

Transaction status change

PL-7QK2M9 · UGX 185,000Awaiting payment
The buyer hasn't paid yet.

Status pills crossfade colour over 220ms; the progress bar eases over 600ms; the new pill scales in once. Failure never shakes. It turns red and explains what to do.

Button feedback

Link created. Copied to clipboard.

Press scales to 97% in 80ms. While working, the label is replaced by a spinner and a verb in progress ("Creating…"), the button keeps its width, and a toast confirms the result.

07 · Responsive

Designed at 375px first

Most buyers will open a Paylink from a chat on a mid-range Android phone, often on a slow connection. Checkout, payment links and tracking are designed for that phone first and then given more room on bigger screens.

Phone · <640One column

Bottom tab bar, sticky bottom action on checkout, bottom sheets for menus and filters, tables become stacked rows. 16px gutters, 44px touch targets.

Tablet · 640–1023Two columns

Checkout stays one centred column (max 560px). The dashboard keeps bottom navigation and shows stats in a 2×2 grid.

Laptop · 1024–1279Sidebar appears

Navy sidebar replaces the tab bar. Tables show in full. Create-link gets its live buyer preview column.

Desktop · ≥1280Capped width

Content stops at 1200px and centres. Extra width goes to whitespace, not to stretched forms; forms stay ≤680px.

Performance budget for the checkout page: under 150 KB of JavaScript on first load, fonts subset to Latin, no images required to complete a payment.

08 · Recommended direction

Calm structure, one green signal, and nothing hidden.

The Interlock mark, Bricolage Grotesque over Instrument Sans, and the emerald-on-navy button as the signature interaction. Product screens stay light and spacious; navy is used for structure (the seller sidebar, headings, the app icon), and emerald appears only where the user acts or where a deal moved forward. A seller should be able to recognise a Paylink screenshot in a WhatsApp chat with the logo cropped out.

What makes it Paylink

  • Emerald labels on navy buttons
  • Shared vertical timeline on every transaction
  • Monospace references like PL-7QK2M9
  • One persistent Demo account bar instead of demo tags everywhere

What it avoids

  • Shields, padlock heroes, banking columns
  • Gradients, glass effects and stock photos
  • Fake metrics or zero-filled dashboards
  • Real-money wording on any screen without the Demo account bar

How Phase 1 uses it

  • Next.js + Tailwind theme generated from tokens.css
  • shadcn/ui primitives restyled with these tokens
  • Lucide icons, Motion for React
  • Light theme ships first; dark tokens are ready

Decisions

  1. Logo · Interlock (B)Selected. Navy and emerald woven frames, lowercase wordmark with an emerald tittle.
  2. Primary button · emerald label on navySelected. 4.78 : 1 contrast; flips to emerald with a navy label on navy surfaces.
  3. Typography · Bricolage Grotesque + Instrument SansApproved.
  4. Demo mode · real-looking screens, account-level noticeSelected. Persistent Demo account bar and avatar badge, a one-time welcome dialog, a notice on checkouts from demo sellers, and a demo outcome panel on the payment step.
  5. DomainDeferred. paylink.ug stays as a placeholder.
