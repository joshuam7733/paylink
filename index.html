Payment links for buyers and sellers who have never met

Paylink

A seller creates a link for one sale. The buyer sees who they are paying, what for and how it will be delivered, pays with mobile money, and both follow the deal on a shared timeline until the buyer confirms it arrived.

This is a look-only preview: screenshots of the working app with fictional demo data. Payments are simulated, and no real money moves.

The website

Built for people who sell on WhatsApp, Instagram and TikTok, and the buyers who don't know them yet.

Creating a link

The seller describes the item, the price and how it will be delivered, then gets one link to share in a chat.

Paying

The buyer sees the seller, the item and the delivery terms before paying with MTN or Airtel mobile money. No account needed. If a payment fails, nothing is taken and they can try again.

Following the deal

Both sides see the same timeline. The deal completes only when the buyer confirms the order arrived.

For sellers

Every sale in one place: what is waiting for payment, what is secured, what failed, and a full history of each transaction.
